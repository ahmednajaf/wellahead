MODIFICATIONS FOR THIS DEMO
Selected external structures; omitted some nested/internal surfaces; added
matching reference vessels around the heart; centred and uniformly scaled;
vertex clustering for smaller meshes; applied illustrative surface colours.
Rendered with perspective, lighting and interactive rotation. Colours are
illustrative. Dimensions are not patient measurements. Some vessel ends are
cropped. These are reference models, not individual scans or diagnostic tools.

Navigation icons: original AI-generated decorative artwork for Well Ahead.
Icon imagery is not a medical diagram or source of medical guidance.
